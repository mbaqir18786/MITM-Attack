Knowledge Base

The MITM Vulnerability

In this lab, the "Attacker" acts as a proxy. Real MITM attacks often use ARP Spoofing to trick your device into sending data through them.

Encoding (Base64) vs Encryption

Observe the "Secure Mode". It uses Base64. This is not encryption; it's just a different way to write the same text. Anyone can decode it.

Why Hashing is Better

In "Hashing Mode", the password itself is never sent. A mathematical fingerprint (hash) is sent instead, making it useless to an interceptor.

The Role of TLS (HTTPS)

HTTPS doesn't just encrypt; it uses Certificates to prove the server is who they say they are, preventing the "Attacker Proxy" from mimicking the server.
